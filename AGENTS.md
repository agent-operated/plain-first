# Repository instructions

- Treat `POLICY.md` as the policy source. Examples and fixtures illustrate it;
  they do not override it.
- Keep Plain First domain-neutral. Do not redefine a producer's outcomes,
  evidence, approval authority, or machine-readable contract.
- Preserve exact formal information while improving its human presentation.
- Never strengthen a claim beyond the underlying evidence.
- Do not add a renderer, schema, checker, registry, service, or runtime
  dependency without an explicit reviewed decision.
- Submit source changes through a pull request and keep policy, examples, and
  fixtures consistent.
